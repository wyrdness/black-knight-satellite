## Critical Evaluation Guide

### When Researching Black Knight Claims

✅ **Do**:
- Check NASA primary sources
- Verify image context and metadata
- Examine timeline of separate events
- Read Duncan Lunan's retraction
- Consider physics of orbital decay

❌ **Don't**:
- Accept viral images without context
- Assume lack of explanation = alien
- Conflate unrelated historical events
- Ignore NASA's identification
- Overlook Lunan's retraction

### Red Flags in Sources

- Claims of "13,000 years" without citation
- Combining all five events as single object
- Ignoring NASA identification of 1998 photo
- Not mentioning Duncan Lunan's retraction
- Presenting thermal blanket photo as mysterious
